I am an M.A. student in political science at Université Laval, in Québec City, supervised by Alexandre Gajevic Sayegh and Yannick Dufresne. I completed my B.A. in political science at the same institution in 2026, with a *profil distinction* and an exchange semester at Sciences Po Lyon. My graduate work is funded by a Canada Graduate Scholarship – Master's (CGS M) from the Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council.

My research sits at the intersection of **political behaviour** and **public opinion**, with a regional focus on **Quebec and Canadian politics** and a growing interest in **activism and social movements**. Methodologically, I work with quantitative and computational tools — survey data, automated text analysis, and large language models applied to political text.

## Research interests
* Quebec and Canadian politics
* Political behaviour and public opinion
* Activism and social movements
* Computational text analysis and large language models in political science

## Current work
I hold research assistantships across several centres at Université Laval:

* **Datagotchi Chatbot** (CLESSN) — outreach and science communication for the tool.
* **Vitrine Démocratique** (CAPP) — automated text analysis and the development of data refiners.
* **Populism and climate change** (OREV) — a snowball literature review.

Two papers are currently in preparation, both on the use of large language models in political science: one on the validity of *silicon sampling* in the Canadian context, and one on the performance of LLMs for sentiment analysis in non-English text. See [Publications](/publications/) for details.

I also teach as a graduate assistant in quantitative methods and research design, and I lead an introductory **R** workshop at Université Laval's interdisciplinary methods school (EIOM).
