A one-hour hands-on introduction to R for graduate students attending Université Laval's interdisciplinary summer methods school (EIOM). The workshop is optional and deliberately informal: the goal is to get participants comfortable opening RStudio, loading data, and running their first lines of code, rather than to cover the language exhaustively.

Facilitator.
